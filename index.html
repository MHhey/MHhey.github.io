PhD research · Under review, 2025

Why ordering swings grow up the supply chain

A steady, predictable order placed on site can reach fabricators and suppliers as a large, expensive swing — surplus material on some projects, shortages on others. I modelled each tier of a construction supply chain as an independent decision-maker working only from what it can see, then measured what changes when tiers share information instead of ordering blind.
