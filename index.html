Supply chain · Doctoral research

Why a small order on site becomes a big problem upstream

Modeled a five-tier construction supply chain — contractor, procurement office, 3PL, distributor and supplier — as autonomous LLM agents, each ordering from only what it can see. Measured how a modest change on site amplifies into large swings upstream, and found that the kind of information shared matters more than simply sharing more of it.
