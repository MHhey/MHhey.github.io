Construction document and imagery pipelines

Built the pipelines at Intellus Build, a vertical SaaS platform for construction and property, that convert permits, drawings, filings, and site photography into structured records for automated reporting.

RoleApplied AI Engineering Intern
StackPython · LLM and agentic frameworks · Computer vision · AWS · SQL
WhenMay 2026 – present

Overview

Project and property records are distributed across permits, filings, drawings and site photography in inconsistent formats, much of it scanned. Downstream reporting requires typed fields. The pipelines close that gap.

Components

Document extraction

  • LLM and agentic stages decompose documents into retrievable units
  • Units routed to the extraction step suited to their content and layout

Computer vision

  • Site imagery processed at scale to roughly estimate construction progress
  • Model development and validation for extracting insights from construction photography

Architecture and data

  • System architecture connecting AI pipelines to backend microservices
  • Storage and retrieval across SQL and AWS, from raw input through deployment
  • REST interfaces between extraction stages and consuming services
